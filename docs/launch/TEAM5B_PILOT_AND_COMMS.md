## 2. Consent, eligibility and joining (drafts, not published)

**Eligibility statement (proposed, pending Team 2 age/control review):**
"This is an experimental, monitored-access StrangerTalks beta for adults aged 18 or older. By participating, you confirm you meet the eligibility policy and understand that you may be connected to another real person whose messages we do not control. Participation is voluntary; you can leave at any time. Do not share your address, passwords, financial information or private documents. Use Block or Report if someone behaves unsafely. Test availability, matches, message delivery and accessibility may be imperfect. The exact Terms and Privacy Notice for this pilot will be supplied before joining. Please do not copy or publish your partner's messages."

**Research consent (separate opt-in):**
"Would you separately agree to answer a short optional feedback survey about whether you understood the experience? No chat transcripts, message quotes, photos or screenshots of another person are requested. You may skip questions or stop. Your answers will be held only for the approved pilot review period and not linked to your anonymous conversation."

An 18+ statement is **not age assurance** by itself. Team 2 must verify actual access safeguards, jurisdiction and policy; if missing, no user contact. Do not assert complete anonymity or permanent erasure. The live 1:1 message path is not an ordinary persisted transcript, but retention choices, local storage, Reports and other durable safety evidence have different lifetimes.

**Join instruction draft:**
"Use the verified private beta link only during the confirmed window. Start in a regular browser tab. Read the safety information, choose one Door that fits what you want to talk about, and join the queue. Matching depends on the presence of another compatible real participant; it is not guaranteed. If waiting becomes inconvenient, you can leave. Please don't share identifying details in a conversation."

## 5. On-duty runbook

### T-72 to T-24 hours (prior authorization mandatory)
- Founder approves beta scope, message, environment, session date/time, maximum attendees, moderator and safety operator.
- Team 2 signs age/consent/Privacy/Terms/Guidelines/Report/Block evidence. Team 1 and Team 4 provide exact deployment SHA and safe rollback/drill proofs.
- Invite and confirm *real* consenting adults only after sign-off. Verify confirmed volunteer availability, not just invites accepted.
- Prepare accessible private link only when actual approved environment is reachable. No open public link until authorized.

### T-2 hours
- On-duty technical operator checks Render deployment + error logs, /health/live, /health/ready, backend DB availability and real two-browser WSS flow on approved SHA.
- Confirm safety reporting route, human reviewer coverage, staff backup and incident/escalation contact route.
- Confirm actual match queue is empty or understood, and no testing identities remain in production. Aggregate measurement freshness indicated; if absent, mark NOT INSTRUMENTED.

### T-15 minutes
- Reconfirm adult eligibility/consent and expected arrivals using **off-product** opt-in tracker. Do not ask for in-chat identity.
- If fewer than founder-approved minimum arrivals can be supported, or safety/operational coverage is absent, cancel. Do not invent attendees.
- Publish honest join instructions only to already-consenting participants.

### T+0 to T+10 minutes
- Admit during concentrated short window if environment approved. Never override queue matching/blocks.
- Log admitted totals and match metrics only if server-authoritative collection exists. Otherwise write NOT INSTRUMENTED, not 0.
- At 5 minutes show honest waiting guidance; allow voluntary cancellation. Contact safety owner immediately on meaningful incident.

### Post-session
- Stop new invitations. Confirm matched/unmatched counts (or telemetry gap), disconnections, Reports, any critical incidents, and last good exact SHA.
- Offer optional private aggregate survey; remind that screenshots/transcripts of other participants are not accepted.
- Complete closure record with unanswered questions and delete restricted consent/contact records on approved timeline.

## 6. Incident escalation and pause policy

Proposed severities pending Team 2/4 signoff:
- **P0 / immediate PAUSE:** identity/token leak, Block bypass, credible imminent harm, abuse reporting path unavailable, unauthorized conversation-content collection, wrong participant boundary, suspected data corruption. Stop invites and new session admissions via authorized operational controls; preserve minimum protected evidence without copying user content; involve Team 2 + Team 1 + founder. Do not make unapproved production changes.
- **P1 / HALT session:** repeated unmatched-error loops, first-message failure, WSS reconnect broken, readiness/down DB, capacity exhaustion, unavailable human reviewer. Stop promoting the window; notify participants honestly; Team 4 owns recovery decision.
- **P2 / track with owner:** confusing copy, low turnout, noncritical UX issue with safe workaround. Record root cause and reopen only with evidence.
