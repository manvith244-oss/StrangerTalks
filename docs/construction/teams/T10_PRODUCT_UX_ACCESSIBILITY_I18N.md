# T10 — Product UX, Accessibility, Localization & Human Comprehension

## Evolution Clause

The interface and terminology may evolve as humans reveal better mental models. UX may clarify product law, but cannot silently redefine backend authority or social meaning.

## Mission

Make StrangerTalks understandable to ordinary humans within seconds, especially where its architecture differs from familiar social media, messaging and dating mental models.

## Owned authority

- user-facing information architecture and interaction clarity;
- accessibility;
- localization/internationalization experience;
- responsive/device UX in coordination with T03;
- first-minute comprehension;
- user-facing safety/privacy explanations;
- terminology presentation for Encounter/Bond/scoped identity/disclosure;
- error/recovery/terminal presentation truth.

## Key mental-model risks

Users may misread:

- anonymous entry as "everyone is fake and unsafe";
- Bond as friend request/follow/dating match/trust certification;
- scoped identity as multiple fake accounts;
- selective disclosure as a platform identity-verification promise;
- private accountability as public reputation;
- temporary/ephemeral behavior as "nothing can ever be retained for safety";
- navigation away as ending a Conversation.

T10 should design comprehension around actual product law rather than borrow familiar labels that import the wrong expectations.

## Accessibility minimums

- keyboard operability;
- visible focus;
- semantic controls/labels;
- reduced motion;
- zoom/reflow;
- sufficient touch targets;
- high-contrast/forced-colors resilience where applicable;
- screen-state announcements for important async changes;
- no safety/error information encoded only by color;
- language/copy that remains understandable after localization.

## Technology posture

- current responsive web UI: ACTIVE NOW;
- Gettext/i18n primitives: ACTIVE;
- native app UX: future trigger, not current assumption;
- design-system extraction: evaluate as UI complexity grows;
- framework rewrite is not a UX objective.

## Evidence

Use real browser journeys, multiple viewport classes, keyboard-only paths, reduced motion/reflow tests, copy/mental-model review and where possible user comprehension studies. Screenshot generation alone is not UX proof.

## Stop conditions

Stop if a UI simplification requires changing Bond meaning, anonymity/disclosure rules, terminal authority, Block/Report behavior or data retention expectations without product approval.
